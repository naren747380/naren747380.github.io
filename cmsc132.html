CMSC132: Object-Oriented Programming II

Course Notes

These notes were written by me while taking CMSC132 at the University of Maryland under Professor Nora Burkhauser. I also used a significant amount of material from Professor Pedram Sadeghian's notes when preparing and expanding these documents.

Some chapters cover topics in greater depth than what may be required for your particular section of CMSC132. This is because the material covered can vary depending on the professor teaching the course. For example, one professor may not cover End-of-File (EOF) handling in File I/O, while another may not cover Kruskal's algorithm in graphs.

I have tried to make these notes as comprehensive as possible by including additional topics and explanations so that students can access the material they need. However, I strongly recommend checking with your professor or referring to your course syllabus to determine which topics you are expected to know. Some documents may contain more material than you need for your exams. Of course, you are welcome to study the additional material if you are interested!

Each document includes a table of contents on its first page. You can click on any chapter below to open the corresponding PDF and see the topics covered in that document. Also if I like made any mistake please inform me at ayinala [AT] gmail [DOT] com and I'll rectify it as soon as possible!
