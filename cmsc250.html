CMSC250: Discrete Structures

Course Notes

These are my notes for CMSC250 at the University of Maryland. I've organized the material into individual chapters, with detailed explanations, worked examples, diagrams, and mathematical proofs to help make the concepts easier to understand.

The notes cover a wide range of topics, including propositional logic, proof techniques, number theory, mathematical induction, set theory, combinatorics, probability, functions, and relations. I've tried to make the material as comprehensive and accessible as possible, particularly for students encountering these concepts for the first time.

Some chapters may cover certain topics in greater depth than what is required for a particular section of CMSC250. Since the topics covered and the level of detail can vary depending on the professor teaching the course, I recommend checking your course syllabus and consulting your professor to determine exactly what you are expected to know.

Each document includes a table of contents. You can click on any chapter below to open the corresponding PDF. If you notice any mistakes or inaccuracies in the notes, please let me know at ayinala [AT] gmail [DOT] com, and I'll do my best to correct them as soon as possible!
