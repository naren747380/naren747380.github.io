CMSC131: Object-Oriented Programming I

Course Notes

These notes were written by me while taking CMSC131 at the University of Maryland in Fall 2025 under Professor Pedram Sadeghian. Professor Sadeghian provided course notes and other instructional materials, which I used as references when preparing and expanding my own notes.

I have tried to make these notes as detailed and accessible as possible, including explanations, examples, and additional information to help students understand the concepts covered in class. Some documents may cover certain topics in greater depth than what is required for a particular section of CMSC131.

Since the topics covered and the level of detail can vary depending on the professor teaching the course, I recommend checking your course syllabus and consulting your professor to determine exactly what you are expected to know. You are, of course, welcome to explore any additional material included in these notes.
