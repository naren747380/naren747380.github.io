CBSE Class 12 Mathematics

Course Notes

These notes were prepared by me while studying Mathematics under the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) Class 12 curriculum in India.

I've tried to make these notes as detailed and accessible as possible, including important formulas, explanations, mathematical concepts, and additional information to help students understand the topics covered in the syllabus.

Some documents may cover certain topics in greater depth than what is required for the CBSE board examinations. I recommend referring to the latest CBSE syllabus and NCERT textbooks to determine exactly what is required for your examinations.
