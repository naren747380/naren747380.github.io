CBSE Class 12 Mathematics

Course Notes

These notes were prepared by me to help students studying Mathematics under the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) Class 12 curriculum in India.

I've tried to make these notes as detailed and accessible as possible, including explanations, important formulas, worked examples, and additional information to help students develop a strong understanding of the concepts covered.

Some documents may cover certain topics in greater depth than what is required for the CBSE board examinations. I recommend referring to the latest CBSE syllabus and NCERT textbooks to determine exactly what is required for your examinations.

You can click on any available chapter below to open the corresponding PDF. If you notice any mistakes or inaccuracies in these notes, please let me know at ayinala [AT] gmail [DOT] com, and I'll do my best to correct them!
